# D1 — Catalan (intermediate → working)

## What it is
Language requirement or nice-to-have for Catalonia-based employers (e.g. Wikiloc).

## Why JDs care
Team communication and customer proximity—not a Kotlin problem.

## Honest closes
- **OWN:** CEFR level + how you use Catalan at work if applicable.
- **SIDE:** Study plan with target exam/date; reflect honestly in CV (“learning”).
- **SKIP:** If role is fully English-operational—confirm in recruiter screen.

## Interview bite
Simple conversational test—never fake fluency.
