# E — Items already in claimed_skills.json

## What this is
Section **E** of `gaps_to_review.md` lists skills already wired into `knowledge_base/cache/claimed_skills.json` (side project / self-study / course). They are **not** Section A gaps.

## Why it matters
Avoid double-counting: you already render these when JD overlap hits. Articles for each live item would duplicate the tracker—**read Section E in the master file** when updating honesty levels.

## Honest closes
- If any entry feels thin, downgrade to `reading` or remove per tracker instructions—**one small decision note** in `gaps_to_review.md` beats another long essay.

## Interview bite
Anything marked self-study must survive “show me the repo / trace / certificate” at the level you claimed.

## Study checklist (before you claim depth)

- Read the **top-level doc pages** above end-to-end once; bookmark the **API/class** pages you would touch in a spike.
- Reproduce **one minimal vertical slice** in a throwaway repo (build, run, capture logs)—evidence beats keywords.
- Write down **three failure modes** (permissions, background, data integrity, or device variance) you could explain without notes.
- Align language with your tracker: **OWN vs SIDE vs SKIP** from `gaps_to_review.md`—interviewers reward precision.
